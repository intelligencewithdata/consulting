Programme 1 · 4 weeks · Live on Google Meet

Excel & AI for Analytics

Turn Excel into a serious analysis tool, then use AI assistants to work faster and smarter.

Who it's for: Beginners and anyone who uses Excel but wants to analyse data with confidence.

  1. Week 1
    Excel foundations for analysis

    Clean, structured data with Tables, sorting, filtering, data validation and the formulas analysts use daily, including XLOOKUP, IF and SUMIFS.

  2. Week 2
    Summarise and transform

    PivotTables, PivotCharts and Power Query in Excel to combine, clean and reshape messy data without manual copy-and-paste.

  3. Week 3
    Dashboards that communicate

    Choose the right charts, build an interactive dashboard with slicers, and present insights clearly.

  4. Week 4
    AI for analytics

    Use AI assistants to write formulas, explain data and draft insights, with prompting techniques, accuracy checks and responsible use. Ends with a capstone project.

Advanced Power BI for Advanced Reporting

Go from Power BI Desktop all the way to publishing, sharing and collaborating in the Power BI Service, the way companies actually use it.

Programme 3 · 4 weeks · Live on Google Meet

SQL for Data Analysis

Learn the language for getting answers out of databases, which almost every data analyst job asks for.

Who it's for: Anyone aiming for a data analyst role, from complete beginners to self-taught learners filling gaps.

  1. Week 1
    SQL fundamentals

    How databases work, then SELECT, filtering with WHERE, sorting and limiting results.

  2. Week 2
    Summarising data

    Aggregations, GROUP BY and HAVING, CASE expressions and cleaning data with SQL.

  3. Week 3
    Combining tables

    INNER and LEFT joins, subqueries and CTEs to break complex questions into clear steps.

  4. Week 4
    SQL for reporting

    Window functions for rankings and running totals, real-world datasets, and a final analysis project.

Questions

Good to know

How are the classes delivered?

Every programme is taught live and remotely on Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere with a laptop and a stable internet connection.

How long is each programme?

Each programme runs for 4 weeks. Taking all three gives you 12 weeks of live training.

Which currency do I pay in?

Fees are set in Ghana cedis (GH₵). The currency switcher shows an estimate in your local currency based on recent exchange rates. The exact amount you pay may vary slightly depending on your bank or payment provider.

What do I need for the Power BI programme?

A Windows laptop is best, because Power BI Desktop runs on Windows. For the Power BI Service part of the course, we set you up with a corporate (work) email account so you can publish, share and collaborate in a real workspace.

Do I need any experience?

No experience is needed for Excel & AI or SQL. For Advanced Power BI, basic Excel skills help. The application form tells us your level so we can guide you.

When do I receive my onboarding materials?

As soon as your payment is confirmed, we email your onboarding pack, class schedule and joining details.
