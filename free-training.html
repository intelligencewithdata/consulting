Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. The Friday sessions are completely free. You just need to register on Luma so we can send you the joining link.

Do I need any experience?

No. Sessions are beginner-friendly, and there's always something for people who already know the basics.

What do I need to join?

A laptop or desktop with an internet connection. Power BI Desktop is free and runs on Windows; Mac users can still follow along and learn the concepts.

What time is it where I live?

Sessions start at 6:00 PM UK time every Friday. The countdown at the top of this page shows the start time in your own time zone.

Can I join any week?

Yes. Join whenever you can. Each session stands on its own, and regular attendance builds your skills week by week.

I want faster progress. Is there more?

Yes. We also offer 1:1 coaching and a simulated data analytics internship. Explore our programmes.
