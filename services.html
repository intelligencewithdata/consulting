Questions

Frequently asked

Do we need Microsoft Fabric, or is Power BI enough?

Many businesses get large gains from Power BI alone. Fabric makes sense when you have several data sources, larger volumes, or want a governed central store. We'll recommend the simplest option that meets your needs during discovery.

How long does a typical automation project take?

It depends on the number of reports and data sources. A focused project automating a handful of core reports often takes a few weeks; we'll give you a timeline with the roadmap.

Can you work with our existing data and systems?

Yes. Power BI connects to most databases, cloud apps, SharePoint and Excel files. We work with what you have and only suggest changes where they clearly pay off.

Is training delivered remotely or on-site?

Both. Most sessions run live online, and we can deliver on-site for teams in the UK.

How do you handle data security?

We work inside your own Microsoft environment with the access you grant, follow UK GDPR principles, and avoid copying data outside your systems.
